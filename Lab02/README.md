## Key Observations

- Overall survival rate was **38.38%**.
- Female survival rate was **74.20%**, compared with **18.89%** for males.
- 1st-class passengers had the highest survival rate at **62.96%**.
- 3rd-class passengers had the lowest survival rate at **24.24%**.
- Female 1st-class passengers had the highest observed survival rate at **96.81%**.
- Male 3rd-class passengers had the lowest observed survival rate at **13.54%**.
- Passengers travelling alone had a survival rate of **30.35%**, compared with **50.56%** for passengers travelling with others.

## Predictions

Based on the patterns in the dataset, female passengers, especially those in 1st class, would generally be expected to have a higher chance of survival. Male passengers in 3rd class would be expected to have the lowest chance of survival.
